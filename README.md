# 🌸 Chiikawa Creator & Village Companion (PC Edition) 🌸

A cute, interactive character creator and cozy village game for PC, inspired by the Chiikawa universe!

## ✨ Features
1. **Interactive 2D Character Creator (100% in English):**
   - **Body:** Slim, Normal, and Chubby body shapes.
   - **Colors:** 16 Preset Pastel swatches + Live Custom Color Picker.
   - **Patterns:** Belly patch, underwear/pants, bib, heart, spots, back stripes.
   - **Face Customization:**
     - 3 Real-time Sliders: Eye Spacing (narrow/wide), Feature Height, Eyebrow Tilt.
     - 6 Eyebrow Styles: Follows expression, soft, maro dots, worried/droopy, fierce, bold.
     - 6 Mouth Styles: Cat smile `( 3 )`, open smile `(Waa!)`, joyful laugh, trembling/crying, cheeky tongue out, innocent dot.
     - 8 Face Markings: Hachiware two-tone parted bangs, eye patch, raccoon mask, star, whiskers, freckles, rosy blush.
   - **Head & Ears (16 Ears):**
     - Classic Chiikawa round bear ears, Usagi tall rabbit ears, Hachiware cat ears, Momonga fluffy ears, puppy ears, sprout ears, horns, antennae, aquatic fin ears, etc.
     - Head accessories: Flower, acorn beret, crown, ribbon, sprout, straw hat.
   - **Dress Up & Weapons:**
     - Acorn Pochette (handmade by Armor Guy!), Hero Cape, Knitted Scarf, Golden Bell Collar, Towel, Bowtie.
     - 15 Tail Types: Cat tail, Momonga giant fluffy tail, fox, dragon, lion, devil, heart, star, beaver, etc.
     - **Weapons:** The iconic **Pink Sasumata** & **Blue Sasumata** pitchforks!
   - **Turn Around (180° Back View):**
     - Character automatically spins to show off tails and back stripes, or toggle manually with the "View Back / View Front" button!
   - **Personality Matrix:**
     - Bravery (Timid ↔ Brave)
     - Sociability (Solitary ↔ Social Butterfly)
     - Lifestyle (Giant Pudding Foodie ↔ High Energy Active)

2. **The Living Village ("Send to Village!"):**
   - **Kusatori Weeding Mini-Game:** Click weeds popping up in the meadow to pull them with a cute "POP!" sound, earning Weeding Points.
   - **Official Weeding License (草むしり検定証):** Generates a personalized Weeding License ID Card with your friend's character photo and name!
   - **Subjugation Quest:** Fight giant beetles using the Sasumata!
   - **Feast Time:** Spawn Giant Pudding or Hot Ramen!
   - **Voice Reactions:** Features authentic Usagi voice clips (*"Ura!"*, *"Yaha!"*, *"Prrrr!"*) and sound effects extracted from the video.
   - **Photo & Sticker Booth:** Export high-resolution transparent PNG avatars and stickers.

## 🎮 How to Run on PC

### Method 1 (Quick Double-Click):
Double-click `Start-Game.bat`. It will automatically launch the game in App Mode in your browser.

### Method 2 (Direct):
Double-click `index.html` to open it in Google Chrome, Microsoft Edge, or any modern web browser.
